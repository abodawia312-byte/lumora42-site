Hi, I'm Mohamed

I'm an independent author, and I make activity books and stories starring a little pink axolotl: mazes, word searches, sudoku, tracing and coloring, plus Pickle, a class axolotl who keeps getting into funny trouble.

I keep the pages big and each task clear, so kids can work on their own and feel proud when they finish. New books join the series through the year, including more Pickle adventures.

Found a mistake or have an idea? Email info@lumora42.shop and I'll read it myself.
